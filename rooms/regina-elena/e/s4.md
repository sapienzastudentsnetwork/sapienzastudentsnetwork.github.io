## **Study Room S4**

{{% hint info %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-location-pin" style="color: #74C0FC;"></i> **Location**

The study room is located on **floor -1** of **Building E** in the Viale Regina Elena complex, accessible from [Viale Regina Elena, 295](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Viale+Regina+Elena,+295,+00161+Roma+RM), or [Via del Castro Laurenziano, 6](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Via+del+Castro+Laurenziano,+6,+00161+Roma+RM).

<i class="fa-solid fa-clock" style="color: #74C0FC;"></i> **Opening Hours**

Open **Monday to Friday**, from **8:00 to 19:30** (during Building E opening hours).

<i class="fa-solid fa-user-check" style="color: #74C0FC;"></i> **Access**

Access is restricted exclusively to **regularly enrolled Sapienza students** for the current academic year.

<i class="fa-solid fa-chair" style="color: #74C0FC;"></i> **Capacity**

The classroom has a capacity of **32** seats (4 tables with 8 seats each).
{{% /hint %}}

{{% hint warning %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-circle-exclamation" style="color: #FFD43B;"></i> **Availability**

The room follows all closures dictated by the **academic calendar**, the **I3S Faculty**, **national holidays**, or whenever Building E is closed for external reasons.
{{% /hint %}}
