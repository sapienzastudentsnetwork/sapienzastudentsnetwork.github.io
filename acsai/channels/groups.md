# Groups

On this page you can find links to the **WhatsApp groups** and **Telegram groups** for the Bachelor's Degree in **Applied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence** (33502 / 30786), also abbreviated to **ACSAI**

## General groups

The main Telegram group is dedicated to general discussions regarding **ACSAI and the application process**, bringing together students from all years as well as prospective applicants.
