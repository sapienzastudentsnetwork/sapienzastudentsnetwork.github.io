## Problems registering on Infostud

{{% hint tip %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb" style="color: #238636;"></i> **Exam Sessions Not Showing on Infostud?**

If you cannot register for an exam through Infostud, **after checking the issue with the lecturer**, you must **send a request to the Educational Affairs Office** at [segr.didattica@di.uniroma1.it](mailto:segr.didattica@di.uniroma1.it).

* **Email Subject:** `Missing Exam in Infostud`
* **Email Content:** make sure to clearly specify:
    * Your **degree programme**
    * The **academic year of your first enrolment**
    * The **course name**
    * The **lecturer** with whom you wish to book the exam

This information is required for the Educational Affairs Office to resolve the issue.
{{% /hint %}}
