# SalvaStudenti

The SalvaStudenti project was created with the goal of offering strategic support to those who are falling behind schedule or facing specific difficulties in passing their **first-year exams**, through a targeted tutoring program.

{{% hint tip %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb" style="color: #238636;"></i> **Who is it for?**

SalvaStudenti is primarily designed for students enrolled in the Bachelor's Degree programs in **Informatica** and **ACSAI** who still need to take the exams required by their first-year study plan, while remaining open to evaluating requests from students with similar needs.
{{% /hint %}}

## How it works

The service is based on the organization of **study groups coordinated by tutors**, with the goal of preparing for exams in a practical and collaborative way.

- **Delivery mode:** meetings are held both online (via digital platforms) and in person, adapting to the operational needs and schedules of the participating students.
- **Methodological approach:** during the tutoring sessions, the most complex theoretical topics are addressed, doubts and particularly critical aspects are clarified, and practical exercises and exam simulations are carried out, fostering continuous interaction between tutors and students.
- **Meeting frequency:** sessions are scheduled on a weekly or periodic basis, offering continuous support in the study of the relevant subjects.

{{% hint warning %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation" style="color: #FFD43B;"></i> **Service availability**

The activation of the tutoring groups is subject to the availability of resources and the tutors involved. While we guarantee the utmost effort in accommodating the requests received, it is not possible to ensure coverage of all participation applications or the activation of tutoring for all the requested courses.
{{% /hint %}}

## How to apply

To join the program, you must fill out the [online request form](https://forms.gle/2AxXr6UjGC4W1tdQ6), specifying in detail which courses you are experiencing the most difficulties with and require support for. Once the applications have been processed, the tutors will structure the groups and provide you with instructions to get started.

{{% hint warning %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation" style="color: #FFD43B;"></i> **Course limit**

Each student can select **a maximum of three courses**.
{{% /hint %}}
