# Study resources

## Notes from the student community

Students enrolled in different academic years have shared their course notes, creating a collection of useful material
for revision and further study. Each student's notes follow their own structure: some are more detailed and extensive,
explaining the entire reasoning process, while others are quicker and more concise 🧙‍♂️

{{% hint warning %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation" style="color: #FFD43B;"></i> **Warning**

Syllabi, course content, and exam methods may change depending on the academic year and lecturer. The completeness
and quality of the notes may also vary: the Sapienza Students Network staff does not systematically verify the material
and cannot guarantee that it is free from errors or typos. You should therefore consult it critically and at your own
risk, always comparing it with the current syllabus and official course material. Many collections are nevertheless of
excellent quality, and some provide practical ways to report possible typos directly to their respective authors.
{{% /hint %}}

{{% hint tip %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb" style="color: #238636;"></i> **Do you have a collection of notes that you would like to make available to everyone?**

## Exercise and Solution Forums

Collaborative spaces organized by course and hosted on GitHub, where students can compare exercises, solutions, and
past exam papers. Visit the dedicated [Exercise and Solution Forums](/compsci/channels/forums/) page.

## Teaching Telegram groups

The [Telegram groups for individual courses](/compsci/channels/groups/#teaching-groups) are useful spaces for
discussing with other students and exchanging information and material related to different courses. Unlike WhatsApp
groups, new Telegram members can also access the full chat history, since messages and shared files are stored in the
cloud. We therefore recommend searching previous messages as well: over time, exercises, explanations, notes, and past
exam papers may have been shared there.

## Official course material and e-learning

At the beginning of each semester, check the [lecturers' noticeboards](https://sapienzastudents.net/sapienzafeed/corsidilaurea/33508/en/teachers.html)
and the [group chats](/compsci/channels/groups/) for updated links to the e-learning pages of individual courses:
Google Classroom, Google Groups, Sapienza e-learning (Moodle), or dedicated course websites. These are normally the
spaces where lecturers publish slides, handouts, exercises, announcements, and other official material during the course.
