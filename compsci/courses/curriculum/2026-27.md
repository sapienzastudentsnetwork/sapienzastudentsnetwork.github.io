## How to compose the study plan

The 120 CFU degree is normally composed as follows:

- **54 CFU** selected from *Group B — Core/characterising courses*;
- **12 CFU** selected from *Group C — Related or complementary courses*;
- **12 CFU of free-choice courses** (*Elective course*), normally split between the first and second year;
- **6 CFU** for the [Complementary Educational Activity (AFC)](/compsci/info/study-plan/afc/);
- **36 CFU** for the Final Exam.

The semester sections below help with scheduling, but they do not indicate by themselves whether a course belongs to Group B or Group C. Consult the Study Plan guide and the official rules when making your selection. In particular, free-choice courses are not limited to the courses listed on this page, provided that the resulting plan is coherent and approved.
