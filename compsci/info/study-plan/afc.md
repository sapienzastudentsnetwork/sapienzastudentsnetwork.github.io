# Complementary Educational Activity (AFC)

{{% hint info %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-circle-info" style="color: #74C0FC;"></i>
The **Complementary Educational Activity (AFC)** is a **6-CFU** activity in the Master's Degree Programme in Computer Science. In Italian it is called *Attività Formativa Complementare*, which explains the acronym **AFC** used in Infostud and in the curriculum.
{{% /hint %}}

## How to obtain the 6 CFU

Students may complete the AFC requirement in one of the following ways:

1. choose one of the AFCs offered each year by the Degree Programme, combining classroom teaching and individual work;
2. carry out a structured activity proposed by a lecturer, involving individual work without classroom teaching;
3. agree with a lecturer on an individual-work topic connected with one of their courses or research activities;
4. pass an exam for a course outside the study plan and have it officially recorded as an AFC;
5. request recognition of an activity completed outside the University from the Committee for the Recognition of Extracurricular Activities.

{{% hint warning %}}
<i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation" style="color: #FFD43B;"></i>
Before starting an individual, external or extra-curricular activity, check the recognition and recording procedure with the lecturer or the competent committee. The activity must ultimately be recorded as the AFC included in your approved study plan.
{{% /hint %}}
